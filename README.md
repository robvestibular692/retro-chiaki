## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to retro-chiaki! This application lets you stream your PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, or PlayStation 5 Pro games directly to your Anbernic handheld device. Whether you're playing on an RG34XXSP (720×480 resolution) or another compatible Anbernic device with the H700 chip, retro-chiaki brings the power of remote play to your portable gaming setup.

## 📋 What You Need

- **Anbernic Device**: Tested on RG34XXSP (720×480) but compatible with other H700 chip devices (640x480 should work too)
- **Supported Operating Systems**: Packaged for muOS and PortMaster
- **PlayStation Console**: PS4, PS5, or PS5 Pro
- **Network**: Stable Wi-Fi connection for streaming

## 🔧 How to Set Up Remote Play

Follow these simple steps to start streaming:

1. **Enable Remote Play on Your PlayStation**:
   - Go to Settings > Remote Play Connection Settings
   - Turn on "Enable Remote Play"
   - Register your device (you'll get a code)

2. **Connect Your Anbernic Device**:
   - Launch retro-chiaki on your Anbernic
   - Enter your PlayStation Network account details
   - Enter the registration code from step 1

3. **Start Streaming**:
   - Select your console from the device list
   - Adjust video quality settings if needed
   - Enjoy your games on the go!

**Q: Does this work with KNULLI?**
A: KNULLI compatibility hasn't been tested yet. We recommend using muOS or PortMaster for now.

**Q: Can I use this on other Anbernic devices?**
A: It's designed for devices with the H700 chip. Tested on RG34XXSP but should work on other compatible models.

**Q: Do I need a PlayStation Plus subscription?**
A: No, remote play is a free feature included with PlayStation consoles.

**Q: Will my controller work?**
A: If your Bluetooth controller is compatible with your Anbernic device, it should work with retro-chiaki.
